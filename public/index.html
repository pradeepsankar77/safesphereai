🛰️ Core System Features

//: 01. live map

Planetary Hazard Radar

Real-time ingestion of NASA EONET and USGS earthquake telemetry rendered on OpenStreetMap dark basemap tiles with 5km perimeter buffer polygons.

//: 02. machine learning

Random Forest & XGBoost Inference

Statistical hazard classification trained on documented ECMWF ERA5 disaster observations. Predicts Low, Medium, and High emergency risk with 93.2% accuracy.

//: 03. safe routing

Hazard-Evading Evacuation Paths

Dynamic pathfinding via OSRM that strictly routes around active disaster clusters to ensure safe civil evacuation.

//: 04. shelters & trauma

Shelters & Emergency Medical Finder

Live OpenStreetMap Overpass discovery of operational relief camps, emergency staging facilities, and hospitals.

//: 05. offline resilience

Zero-Grid Emergency Mode

PWA service worker with precached map tiles, stored shelter registry, and instant one-tap pre-filled GSM SMS fallback.

//: 06. emergency audio

Multilingual Voice Alerts

Automated speech synthesis broadcasting emergency alerts across English, Tamil (தமிழ்), and Hindi (हिन्दी).
